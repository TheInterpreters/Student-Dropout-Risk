## Real predictive findings

These results are from a **refit using the recorded settings and verified checkpoint**. The team's private original fitted-model/freeze bundle was not supplied, so identity with that original serialized model is not established.

| Metric on all 544 validation rows | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| ROC-AUC | 0.9521 |
| Brier score | 0.0708 |
| Expected calibration error, 10 bins | 0.0186 |
| Balanced accuracy under the capacity policy | 75.35% |
| Precision among the 108 outreach flags | 100.0% |
| Recall of the 213 recorded dropout cases | 50.70% |

Under the frozen top-20% outreach policy, 108 students (19.85% of 544) are flagged. All 108 have recorded Dropout outcomes: **TP=108, FP=0, FN=105, TN=331**. The model ranks validation risk well, but the limited outreach policy leaves 105 of 213 dropout cases unflagged. The observed 100% precision is a result on this sample, not a guarantee for future students. These flags are a cohort-capacity rule, not classification at a universal 0.5 probability threshold.

Predictive discrimination and calibration do not establish explanation faithfulness, causal effects, fairness or deployment readiness. No model tuning or final-test scoring was performed using these results.

## What is still missing from a full audit

Every validation student still needs a complete genuine case record containing local signed grouped SHAP, ranked-versus-random deletion, ten selected-group pair probes, six surrogate comparisons, ten seed/background repetitions, eligible grade-perturbation checks and the configured support/scenario analysis. Completion requires exactly 544 distinct original validation IDs, unchanged input/checkpoint/runtime hashes, and successful full-report generation. No population-level explanation-quality findings are claimed from unfinished cases.

The original inference path repeatedly recomputes training context. Its first actual SHAP calculation took about 45 seconds, before many other per-student repetitions and model calls. The runner now offers the library-supported KV inference cache with explicit checks against uncached predictions on **all 544 validation rows plus 32 reference-masked probes**. The declared absolute numerical tolerance is `1e-4` probability (0.01 percentage points), 100 times smaller than the frozen prediction-preserving perturbation tolerance. Validation outreach membership must match exactly. This bounds numerical agreement; it is not bitwise identity or a proof over every possible input. Cache agreement is recorded separately and case outputs use a distinct recorded runtime. The interrupted uncached zero-case manifest was archived; no completed case evidence was discarded or mixed.
